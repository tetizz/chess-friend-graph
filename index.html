READING THE BOARD

A snapshot.
Not the whole story.

Counts describe public observations. They do not measure friendship quality, online activity, request acceptance, or who added whom.

Download the public dataset ↓
What is included?

Saved public Chess.com accounts, friend counts, and ratings. Roster coverage and friend-count coverage are measured separately. A saved collection is partial and does not establish an instantaneous or exhaustive census.

Exact, lower bound, or unknown?

An exact count records the observed total at its observation time. A lower bound means at least that many friends; an arrow continues upward because its upper total is unknown. Unknown values are never treated as zero and remain visible in the table.

How do ratings work?

Bullet, Blitz, and Rapid are saved Chess.com ratings. Overall is the arithmetic average of all three, available only when all three are known. It is a descriptive comparison, not an official Chess.com rating. Points need both a known count and the selected rating.

How current are the observations?

The dataset save time, each observation time, and the date of the last rated game are different. Older ratings may remain listed. A counted public list covers a traversal period and is not an atomic current account total.
